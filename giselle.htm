Gillian Murphy in Giselle

Gillian Murphy as the Queen of the Wilis in Giselle, photo by Rosalie O'Connor. Gillian Murphy is a principal dancer with the American Ballet Theatre. She is a native of Seattle, Washington, and joined the company in 1997. She was promoted to soloist in 2000 and to principal dancer in 2002. She has danced leading roles in many of the company's productions, including Giselle, Swan Lake, and The Sleeping Beauty.
